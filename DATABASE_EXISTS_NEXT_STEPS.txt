================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Problem: "connection refused" or "could not connect to database"
Solution:
  1. Make sure start-postgresql.bat completed
  2. Make sure start-redis.bat completed
  3. Wait 5 seconds after starting services
  4. Try again

Problem: "port 8085 already in use"
Solution:
  1. Close other instances of WhyLock
  2. Or wait a minute after stopping
  3. Then try run-now.bat again

Problem: "Redis connection timeout"
Solution:
  1. Make sure start-redis.bat completed
  2. Keep the Redis window open
  3. Restart Redis
  4. Try again
